How to read this page. One block of the chain, as this node stores it.

Hash
identifies the block; it covers every header field and the Merkle root.
Previous hash
the link to the parent block. It is what makes a chain: change one block and every later hash changes.
Nonce, Difficulty
proof of work: the miner tried nonces until the hash met the difficulty.
Validator, Signature
used by proof of authority; "none" in proof of work.

The first transaction of a mined block is the coinbase: it has no inputs and creates the miner's reward.
